# Secondary Binding Dormitory Replacements

Primary replacement validation uses configured membership in the selected group, including a replacement operator’s secondary bindings. A dormitory primary and working primary in that group retain their identities and fixed recovery tracking through off-shift and return arrangements. Cross-group, self and working-to-working primary replacements remain invalid.
