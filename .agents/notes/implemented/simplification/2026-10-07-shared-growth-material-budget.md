# Shared growth material budget

Recommendation previews and automatic preparation share growth material entries and MaterialBudget for promotion, skills and every selected module stage. Separate presentation groups preserve the single aggregate budget; crafting fees enter that budget once. Duplicate decomposition and whole-queue readiness paths have no external consumers and are removed. Existing legacy recommendation APIs remain compatible for callers. The focused growth and mastery-material tests verify prerequisites and shared stock.
