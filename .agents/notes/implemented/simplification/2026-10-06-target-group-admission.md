# Target Group Admission and Active Covers

Group dormitory capacity takes the target group and resolves its configured followers and per-binding replacements in one place. Backup transitions compare the actual cover with the preserved active binding. Changes to inactive columns do not recall a valid resting operator; removing or changing the active binding retains ordinary correction.
